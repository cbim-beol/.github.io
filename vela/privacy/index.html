Vela privacy policy Vela is a private application used solely by Olivier Béro to access his own Google account data, such as his Gmail mailbox. Vela reads mail metadata and content only to assist its owner, for example to search, summarise and organise his own mail. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Vela's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account security settings.
